VOTEDEX

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

The short version: VoteDex has no accounts and does not collect, sell or share your personal information. There are no ads and no tracking.

WHAT WE COLLECT

Nothing that identifies you. VoteDex doesn't ask for your name, email or phone number, and you never sign in.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

Your choices are saved on your own device and never sent to us. That includes your home state, your districts, how you voted on bills, the topics you follow, and your alert settings. Clearing your browser or app data erases them.

ICLOUD (IPHONE APP)

In the iPhone app, a copy of those same choices is also saved in your own iCloud account, so your state and votes show up on your other Apple devices. This copy is stored by Apple under your Apple Account. VoteDex can't see it, and nobody else can either. If you aren't signed in to iCloud, your choices stay only on that device. Clearing your votes or changing your state in the app updates the iCloud copy too. See Apple's privacy policy.

FINDING YOUR DISTRICTS

If you type your address and tap FIND, it goes straight from your device to the U.S. Census Bureau's free address service, which returns your districts. VoteDex never sees or stores your address. You can skip this and pick your districts from a list. See the Census Bureau privacy policy.

NOTIFICATIONS

Alerts are off unless you turn them on. Right now your alert choices are only saved on your device, and nothing is sent. If VoteDex starts sending alerts, it will only use what it needs to deliver them to your device. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in your device settings.

NO TRACKING OR ADS

VoteDex has no analytics, ad networks, tracking pixels or cookies of its own.

SERVICES WE USE

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web host, it may keep basic server logs, such as IP addresses. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts supplies the lettering. Your device downloads the fonts from Google. See Google's privacy policy.

WHERE THE INFORMATION COMES FROM

Everything VoteDex shows is public record: bills from Congress.gov, votes from the House Clerk and the U.S. Senate, campaign money from the Federal Election Commission (FEC), member details and photos from the public congress-legislators project, and state lawmakers from Open States. Donor amounts and employers are copied from public FEC filings.

CHILDREN

VoteDex collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

CONTACT

Questions or concerns? Open an issue on GitHub.
